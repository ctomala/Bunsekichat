# Research cohort design

Cohorts provide a stable, non-PII analytical dimension independent of operational parallel. Future research views should use a student pseudonym, teacher pseudonym, subject code, academic-period code, course code, parallel code, and cohort code. The stored `research_group` can express an approved study grouping but must not carry identity.

Later measures may add mastery, attempts, response time, confidence, misconceptions, interventions, progress velocity, and dropout-risk signals under separate design and consent review. Exact GPS is excluded from research exports by default; any approved analysis uses derived measures such as distance band, sector, travel burden, or location stability. This phase makes no claim of consent migration and does not change existing GPS behavior.
